# People Research — Public Interest Only

This workflow is for verifying public duties, professional history, published work, litigation, sanctions, or conflicts of interest relevant to a defined public-interest question. Keep the result tied to that question and avoid unrelated personal details.

## Official notices

If the question concerns a purported official notice, use
[official-notice-verification.md](official-notice-verification.md) to check its
issuing authority, public case identifier, publication date, updates, and current
status. Separate allegations from adjudicated findings and distinguish namesakes.

Neither an official notice nor a public-interest rationale authorizes locating a
private person, building a private-life dossier, or contacting the subject.

## Stages

1. Define the public-interest question and excluded personal data.
2. Resolve identity using name, public role, organization, publication identifiers, jurisdiction, and dates. Use professional context, not private addresses or facial matching.
3. Prioritize official biographies, filings, professional licenses, court/regulatory records, publications, and direct statements.
4. Use reputable reporting and archives for context.
5. Distinguish verified identity from namesakes; an identical name or handle is only a lead. Preserve incompatible dates, roles, and jurisdictions as contradictions.
6. Check source lineage and distinguish a person's own statement from independent corroboration. Time-index mutable roles and affiliations.
7. Redact home addresses, private contacts, relatives, and unrelated personal history. Report only the minimum public professional information supporting the claim.

## Do not

- Identify private people by face.
- Locate private people or track their movements or routines, including people named in official notices.
- Aggregate unrelated accounts into a personal dossier.
- Infer sensitive personal traits from profiles, writing, affiliations, or social connections.
- Contact or impersonate the subject, relatives, colleagues, or other third parties.
- Investigate minors or vulnerable individuals.
