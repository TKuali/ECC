# Investigation Case Notes

Use only sections needed for the case. Keep records in the user's workspace,
reference evidence IDs instead of duplicating notes, and omit unrelated personal
data. All dates reflect actual evidence; use unknown where necessary.

## Scope

- Exact question and decision supported:
- Target type and corroborated identifiers:
- Required date/interval and geography:
- Requested granularity/viewpoint/output format:
- Authorization or public-interest basis:
- Allowed sources/actions and excluded data:
- Available artifacts, budget, and stop condition:

## Primary-Evidence Queue

| ID | URL/file and relevant time | Why it matters | Status | Checks/transforms and outputs | Could overturn which claim? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Statuses: unprocessed / processed / blocked / irrelevant. Record why an item is
blocked or excluded. Keep alternate reproducible payloads as separate evidence.

## Hypothesis Ledger

| ID | Claim | Status/confidence | Support | Contradictions | Unprocessed dependencies | Source lineage | Fastest falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Statuses: open / leading / contradicted / verified. State the executable
comparison and expected result, not only a general research question.

## Strategy Checkpoint

- Current evidence state and exact unresolved question:
- Leading anchor and accumulated untested assumptions:
- New evidence/change since last checkpoint, or none:

| Candidate action | Question tested | Representation/habitat | Expected result and effect | Cost/access/fidelity/risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- Selected action and why it outranks alternatives:
- Pending cheaper falsifier and reason deferred:
- Actual result, evidence ID, and confidence/candidate change:
- Next decisive check or stopping reason:

For repeated searches, record query family, representation, habitat, source
clusters, and actual progress. A new query alone is not progress.

## Evidence and Source Assessment

Use `evidence-ledger.csv` or the helper for structured finding records.

| ID | Claim/label/confidence | Source URL/file, title, publisher | Relevant date/access time | Supporting note | Lineage | Limits/contradictions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Assess original versus copy, direct access to the fact, source class, incentives,
method transparency, independent corroboration, and what is not established.
Record local-file hashes and transformations when relevant. Hashes establish
byte identity, not truth. Import raw CSV as text, never executable formulas.

## Time and Deferred Clues

| Evidence ID | Required time | Publication/content | Capture/upload/event | Archive/access | Match or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Clue ID | Raw observation/source | Uncertainty | Possible relevance | Revisit trigger | Status/reason |

Deferred statuses: parked / activated / resolved / discarded.
For a reusable method, record problem signature, prerequisites, method,
falsifier, verification contract, failures, and limits. Persist only if requested.

## Falsification and Readiness

- Strongest contradiction and test performed:
- Closest credible alternative and result:
- Primary evidence/transformations resolved or explicitly bounded:
- Entity, time, source independence, and required geometry checked:
- Could any unread primary item still overturn the answer?
- State: collecting / hypothesizing / falsifying / converged / reopened:
- Definitive conclusion ready? If no, provisional result and next discriminator:

If an answer is challenged, record exact feedback, claim versus format failure,
last verified checkpoint, affected hypotheses, one assumption changed, and retest.

## Final Report

1. Answer, confidence, and provisional/definitive status.
2. Scope, relevant date, authorization, and limitations.
3. Material findings with evidence IDs and direct citations.
4. Source-lineage note and strongest falsification result.
5. Timeline, relationships, or candidate comparison if useful.
6. Contradictions, unresolved originals, and what remains unknown.
7. Methods, tools, handling notes, and next decisive action.

Finding labels: verified fact / corroborated inference / open hypothesis /
single-source lead / contradicted / unknown. A budget stop permits a provisional
report; do not invent certainty to fill the requested output.
